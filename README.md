## 🌟 Overview
Enterprise teams spend an average of 4.5 hours manually reviewing Master Service Agreements (MSAs) and vendor contracts. Over 68% of small-to-midsize businesses unknowingly sign agreements containing uncapped indemnification, broad IP expropriation, and asymmetric liability caps.

**DocuClear AI** parses complex enterprise legal documents in seconds, flags hidden liabilities through an interactive **Red-Flag Heatmap**, and generates balanced, legally protective counter-clauses with one-click export.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Deterministic Clause Classification:** Uses strict JSON schema enforcement to classify terms into *Critical*, *Warning*, and *Safe* tiers with zero hallucination.
- **Visual Risk Heatmap:** Highlights exact toxic language (e.g., unilateral indemnity, unlimited liability) alongside computed risk telemetry.
- **1-Click Balanced Counter-Clauses:** Instantly suggests industry-standard mutual indemnities and trailing 12-month liability caps.
- **Offline / Local Fallback Architecture:** Engineered with cached payload backups to maintain instant responsiveness under zero-connectivity environments.
